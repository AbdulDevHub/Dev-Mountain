## Asset Filenames – Quick Copy

This page exists purely for **easy copy-pasting** of commonly used asset filenames.

---

## 📁 Animation & Media

```text
animejs-v3-header-animation.gif
Snap Scrolling.png
````

---

## 🎨 SVG Assets

```text
background.svg
cursor.svg
dark.svg
float.svg
light.svg
loader.svg
pointer.svg
```

---

## 🖼️ Images & Icons

```text
faviconV2.png
```

---

## 🧠 Notes

* Filenames are **case-sensitive**
* Spaces are preserved where applicable
* Ideal for quick use in `img src`, CSS, or imports
